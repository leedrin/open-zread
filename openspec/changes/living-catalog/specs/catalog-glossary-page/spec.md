## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: 专属术语表页面
The catalog SHALL include a dedicated glossary page with a stable `id`/slug (e.g. `glossary`), visible in the sidebar, that presents the unified glossary. The page's `docType` SHALL be `reference`.

#### Scenario: Glossary page present in catalog
- **GIVEN** a project with a non-empty glossary
- **WHEN** the catalog is finalized
- **THEN** a dedicated glossary page exists and appears in `_sidebar.md`

#### Scenario: Stable identity across regenerations
- **WHEN** the catalog is regenerated
- **THEN** the glossary page keeps the same stable `id`/slug

### Requirement: 确定性渲染术语表与双向映射
The glossary page content SHALL be rendered deterministically (not LLM-authored) from `glossary[]` and each page's `concepts`, during the finalize stage. It SHALL contain:

- one entry per glossary term: canonical name, aliases, definition
- a bidirectional term ↔ page mapping: each term links to its authoritative page (`canonicalPage`), and lists every page whose `concepts` include that term ("appears in")

#### Scenario: Term entry rendered
- **GIVEN** a glossary term with aliases and a definition
- **WHEN** the glossary page is rendered
- **THEN** the page shows the canonical name, its aliases, and the definition

#### Scenario: Bidirectional mapping rendered
- **GIVEN** term "技能系统" with `canonicalPage` P and two other pages whose `concepts` include "技能系统"
- **WHEN** the glossary page is rendered
- **THEN** the term links to P and lists all pages that cover it

#### Scenario: No LLM and no hallucination
- **WHEN** the glossary page is produced
- **THEN** it is generated by deterministic rendering only (no Agent/LLM call, no tokens consumed)

### Requirement: 术语/概念变化时刷新
The glossary page SHALL be refreshed during finalize when the glossary or any page's `concepts` change, staying in sync with the catalog. When the glossary page is `locked`, it SHALL NOT be refreshed (consistent with the hard-lock guarantee).

#### Scenario: Refresh on glossary change
- **GIVEN** a new term was added to the glossary
- **WHEN** finalize runs
- **THEN** the glossary page reflects the new term

#### Scenario: Locked glossary page not refreshed
- **GIVEN** the glossary page is `locked`
- **WHEN** finalize runs after a glossary change
- **THEN** the locked glossary page content is left unchanged
